### Model 1: `budget_cash_flow`

Use when user ask about income, spending, budgets, recurring expenses, changes in ongoing financial commitments, and ongoing affordability.

Common signals:

- Frequency: monthly, weekly, biweekly, every two weeks, twice monthly, quarterly, annually, recurring
- Expense types: rent, utilities, subscriptions, memberships, groceries, transportation, insurance
- Intent: determining whether ongoing expenses fit a budget, calculating remaining income after expenses, or evaluating a recurring financial change

Examples:

- Can I afford a $70/month gym membership?
- What happens if my rent increases by $200/month?
- How much money will I have left after monthly expenses?
- Can I reduce my spending by $300/month?

A recurring payment normally belongs to `budget_cash_flow`, not `savings_goal`.

### Model 2: `savings_goal`

Use for one time purchases, savings targets, future savings projections, and determining how long it takes to reach a financial goal.

Common signals:

- Purchases: laptop, vacation, furniture, electronics
- Savings targets: save $3,000, reach a goal, accumulate savings
- Intent: purchase affordability, required monthly savings, time to goal, projected savings

Examples:

- Can I afford a $1,200 laptop?
- How much should I save monthly to reach $3,000 by June?
- How long until I can afford a $2,000 vacation?
- How much will I have saved after six months?

Monthly contributions toward a savings goal still belong to `savings_goal`.

### Model 3: `bill_split`

Use for dividing shared expenses, allocating costs among participants, reimbursements, and settlements.

Common signals:

- Split, divide, share, reimburse, who owes whom
- Equal splits, itemized splits, weighted allocation
- Shared restaurant bills, trips, utilities

Examples:

- Split a $120 dinner bill among four people.
- How much does each roommate owe for utilities?
- I paid for dinner. How much should my friends reimburse me?

A recurring expense still belongs to `bill_split` when the intent is allocating costs among people.

### Model 4: `debt_repayment`

Use for debt balances, loan repayments, interest calculations, monthly payments, and payoff timelines.

Common signals:

- Loan, credit card debt, outstanding balance, APR, interest, repayment
- Pay off, monthly payment, payoff deadline
- Intent: calculating interest, required payments, total repayment, or payoff duration

Examples:

- How long will it take to pay off my $5,000 loan?
- What monthly payment would pay off my debt in 12 months?
- How much interest will I pay?

Monthly payments associated with debt repayment should not automatically be classified as `budget_cash_flow`.

### Routing Rules

- Select based on financial intent and context, not keywords alone.
- Distinguish recurring expenses from one time purchases.
- Do not classify every question containing "afford" as `savings_goal`.
- Return `gap` when no model fits.
- Return `ambiguous` when clarification is necessary.
- Return `multiple` with the applicable model IDs when a question explicitly involves multiple models.
- Do not select factors or extract variables in this stage.

Example:

```json
{"model": "budget_cash_flow"}
```


For multiple return {"model":"multiple","models":[...]} with at least two distinct applicable model IDs. For ambiguous return {"model":"ambiguous","clarification":"one concise question about financial intent"}. For gap return {"model":"gap"}. Return ONLY this routing JSON, with no other fields.
